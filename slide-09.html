Write Instructions, Not Commandments

The instinct is to control the model with rules. Resist it.

Today's models reason well. They follow intent better than they follow a stack of all-caps MUSTs. When you explain why a step matters, the model handles cases you never wrote down.

Weak body
Strong body
"ALWAYS use a table. NEVER use prose. ALWAYS include 6 rows."
"Use a table so the client can scan status in seconds; prose buries the signal.the why"
Rigid and brittle
Reasoned and adaptable
Breaks on edge cases
Generalises to new ones
Use the imperative voice, but earn it with reasoning. A rule the model understands is a rule it applies well.
